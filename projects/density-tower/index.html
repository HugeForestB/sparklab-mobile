1. Background and hypothesis

Density is a physical property

Density tells how much mass is packed into a certain volume. A pure substance has a characteristic density at a given temperature.

density = mass ÷ volume

Mass, weight, and volume

Mass is the amount of matter. Weight is the force of gravity. Volume is the space matter takes up. Density uses mass and volume.

How layers form

When liquids remain separate, more-dense liquids move below less-dense liquids. Pouring order does not determine the final order.

Variables

The independent variable is what changes. The dependent variable is what is observed or measured.

Model note: The simulation isolates density. Real boundaries may become blurry if liquids are shaken or mixed.
